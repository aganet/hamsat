OrbitQTH: real-time amateur radio satellite tracker

OrbitQTH shows the live position of every amateur radio satellite on a world map with day and night shading. Set your station location as coordinates or a Maidenhead locator, or pin it directly on the map, and the page computes azimuth, elevation, range, range rate and Doppler shift for the satellite you select, together with pass predictions for the next 24 hours and a polar sky view of the current or next pass.

Orbits are propagated with SGP4 from two-line element sets published by AMSAT and CelesTrak, refreshed every six hours. Everything runs in your browser: there is no account, no tracking and no server-side computation. The transponder table lists uplink and downlink frequencies, CTCSS tones and beacons for the satellites you can actually work, so you can tune for a pass without leaving the page.

What the tracker shows

  • Live sub-satellite point, altitude, orbital speed and footprint radius.
  • Footprint circles, ground track, and a great-circle line from your station.
  • Azimuth, elevation, range, range rate and Doppler shift for the listed downlink.
  • Pass predictions with AOS, LOS, maximum elevation and duration.
  • A six-hour timeline of upcoming passes across all tracked satellites.
  • Twilight terminator, sun and moon sub-points, and satellite sunlight or eclipse state.
